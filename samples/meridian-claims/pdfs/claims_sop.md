# Meridian Claims Co. — Standard Operating Procedure (FICTIONAL)

The documented claims process. Controls are mandatory unless an exception is logged.

1. **Claim Intake** — Intake Clerk records the claim.
2. **Triage & Assignment** — Triage Specialist assigns severity and owner.
3. **Document Review** *(control)* — Adjuster verifies coverage and damages. **Required for every claim.**
4. **Manager Approval** *(control)* — Claims Manager signs off; a sign-off artifact must be attached.
5. **Payment** — Finance disburses.
6. **Exception Handling** — Adjuster resolves flagged claims; returns them to review.
7. **Closure** — Intake Clerk closes the claim.

Canonical status values: `Open`, `In Progress`, `Approved`, `Paid`, `Closed`.
